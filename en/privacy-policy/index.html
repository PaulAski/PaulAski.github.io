This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use Yearborne: Life Simulator ("the App"). The App is a mobile life simulation game.

1. Who is responsible for the App

The publisher of the App is:

A-SCI Pawel Adamski

Contact email: paweladamsky96@gmail.com

For privacy-related questions, you can contact us at the email address above.

2. Information processed by the App

The App may process the following types of information.

2.1 Gameplay data

The App may process gameplay-related information, including:

  • character name entered by the user,
  • selected gender,
  • selected country,
  • birth year,
  • avatar and portrait options,
  • game progress,
  • life history,
  • in-game choices,
  • in-game statistics,
  • in-game currency,
  • virtual rewards,
  • achievements,
  • relationships with simulated characters,
  • education and career progress,
  • health and lifestyle choices,
  • financial and asset-related game progress,
  • dynasty and family-tree progress,
  • save slots,
  • autosave data,
  • settings and preferences.

This information is used to run the game, save progress, restore saves, personalize gameplay, display game results and maintain game functionality.

The App is a simulation game. You should not enter real sensitive personal information as your character name or in any gameplay field.

2.2 Save data and local storage

The App may store game progress and settings locally on your device.

This may include:

  • autosave data,
  • manual save slots,
  • current game state,
  • character progress,
  • in-game relationships,
  • in-game history,
  • achievements,
  • settings,
  • notification preferences,
  • rewarded ad reward history,
  • timing information for future in-game sponsored opportunities.

This data is used to continue or restore gameplay.

If you delete a save slot, clear app data or uninstall the App, locally stored progress may be deleted.

2.3 Settings and device features

The App may store or use settings related to:

  • appearance mode,
  • haptic feedback,
  • confirmation prompts,
  • detailed effect display,
  • notification frequency,
  • rewarded ad actions,
  • save management.

The App may use device haptics to provide vibration feedback on supported devices. Haptic feedback can be disabled in the App settings.

2.4 Notifications

The App may ask for permission to send notifications.

If enabled, notifications may be used to remind you to continue your game, return to a pending decision or check your character's situation.

Notification settings may include:

  • whether notifications are enabled,
  • reminder frequency,
  • notification permission status.

You can disable notifications in the App settings or in your device system settings.

The App does not require notifications to function.

2.5 Advertising-related data

The App uses or may use Google AdMob to display ads, including rewarded ads. Google and its advertising partners may process certain information in order to provide advertising, measure ad performance, prevent fraud and abuse, and comply with applicable laws.

Advertising-related data may include, depending on device settings, user consent, Google settings and applicable law:

  • advertising identifiers,
  • device identifiers,
  • IP address,
  • approximate location derived from IP address,
  • device and app information,
  • ad interaction data,
  • diagnostics and performance data,
  • consent choices,
  • information used for ad measurement,
  • information used for fraud prevention.

We do not directly control all data processed by Google AdMob. Google's processing is governed by Google's own policies and user settings.

You can learn more about how Google uses information from apps that use its services here:

https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

3. Rewarded ads

The App may offer rewarded ads. This means that you may choose to watch an advertisement in exchange for an in-game reward.

Rewarded ads are optional. If you do not want to watch a rewarded ad, you can continue using the App without receiving the specific reward connected to that ad.

Rewards from rewarded ads may include virtual in-game benefits such as:

  • in-game cash,
  • temporary stat boosts,
  • wellness-related bonuses,
  • learning-related bonuses,
  • random sponsored opportunities.

These rewards are part of the game only. They are not real money, have no cash value and cannot be exchanged for real-world money, goods or services.

The App may store information that a rewarded ad reward was granted, such as reward type, in-game year and reward description. This is used only to maintain game progress and reward history.

4. Personalized and non-personalized ads

Depending on your location, consent choices, device settings and applicable law, the App may show:

  • personalized ads,
  • non-personalized ads,
  • limited ads,
  • or no ads if ads cannot be requested.

Personalized ads may use information such as advertising identifiers, app activity, device information and other signals permitted by your consent and applicable law.

Non-personalized ads are not based on your advertising profile, but they may still use contextual information, approximate location, device information, frequency capping, aggregated reporting and fraud prevention.

For users in regions where consent is required, including the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, the App may ask for consent before requesting certain types of ads.

5. Consent management

The App may use Google's User Messaging Platform or another consent management mechanism to request, store and update your advertising consent choices where required by law.

You may be asked whether you consent to the use of your personal data for advertising purposes, including personalized ads and related processing.

Your choices may affect the type of ads shown in the App.

You may be able to change or withdraw your consent through the consent form shown in the App, device settings, Google settings or other privacy controls made available by Google or the App.

Depending on your location and applicable law, information may be processed on one or more of the following legal bases:

10. Data sharing

We may share or make available limited information to third-party service providers when necessary for the App to function.

In particular, advertising-related information may be processed by Google AdMob and Google's advertising partners.

We do not sell your personal information.

We do not provide user account databases to advertisers.

11. Data retention

Gameplay data stored locally on your device is retained until you delete it, clear app data or uninstall the App.

Manual save slots remain until you overwrite or delete them.

Autosave data remains until replaced, cleared, removed by the App, deleted by the user or removed with app data.

Advertising-related data processed by Google AdMob is retained according to Google's own policies and settings.

If you contact us by email, we may retain your message for as long as needed to respond, handle support requests, maintain records and comply with legal obligations.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.
